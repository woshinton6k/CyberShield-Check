About Our Mission

Zero-Knowledge Security Utilities & Independent Threat Research

CyberShield Check (cybershieldcheck.com) was founded and is maintained by Mr. Soulwizard, a Cybersecurity Defense Specialist, to give individuals, remote teams, and small businesses fast, transparent tools to evaluate their digital security posture without handing sensitive passwords or personal identifiers to external servers.

Every cryptographic entropy calculation, URL dissection, and personal risk audit on this platform runs 100% locally inside your web browser's memory using client-side JavaScript.

Our technical guides and security benchmarks are aligned with NIST SP 800-63B digital identity guidelines, CISA cyber hygiene recommendations, and open RFC standards for email authentication (SPF, DKIM, and DMARC).
