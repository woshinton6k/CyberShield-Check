CRYPTOGRAPHY • 4 MIN READ

Understanding Password Entropy: Why Length Beats Complexity

Password entropy measures the mathematical unpredictability of a secret string in bits. While traditional policies forced users to substitute "@" for "a" or add a trailing exclamation mark, modern cracking clusters using hashcat and high-end GPUs test predictable substitutions in milliseconds.

The formula E = L × log₂(R) proves that increasing length (L) scales search space exponentially faster than expanding the character pool (R). A random 4-word Diceware passphrase achieves over 75 bits of entropy while remaining easy for humans to memorize.

AUTHENTICATION • 5 MIN READ

Phishing-Resistant MFA: FIDO2 Passkeys vs. SMS One-Time Codes

Not all Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) methods offer equal protection. SMS-based codes are vulnerable to SIM-swapping attacks and SS7 interception, while standard authenticator apps can still be bypassed by Adversary-in-the-Middle (AiTM) reverse-proxy phishing kits.

To eliminate credential theft, security engineers recommend FIDO2/WebAuthn hardware keys or device-bound passkeys. Because cryptographic keys are bound to the legitimate domain origin, they will never authenticate on a lookalike phishing URL.

THREAT DEFENSE • 4 MIN READ

Preventing Credential Stuffing & Data Breach Cascades

When a third-party retailer or forum suffers a database breach, billions of email-and-password pairs are aggregated into automated credential stuffing lists. Attackers use botnets to test those exact combinations across banking, cloud storage, and primary email accounts.

Isolating every online service with a unique, randomly generated password stored inside an encrypted zero-knowledge password manager ensures that a single corporate breach never compromises your wider digital identity.
