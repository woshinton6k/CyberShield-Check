GUIDE 01 • CRYPTOGRAPHY & AUTHENTICATION

Password Entropy vs. Complexity: Why Length Mathematically Defeats GPU Cracking Clusters

For decades, corporate IT policies forced users to create passwords like P@ssw0rd!23—eight to ten characters mixing uppercase letters, numbers, and symbols. Modern offline hash-cracking hardware has rendered those rules obsolete. Specialized rigs equipped with multiple NVIDIA RTX 4090 or enterprise H100 GPUs can evaluate over 150 billion NTLM or MD5 hashes per second, and hundreds of millions of salted SHA-256 guesses every second.

Cryptographic strength is governed by information entropy, measured in bits using the formula E = L × log₂(R), where L is password length and R is the size of the character pool. Adding a single symbol to an 8-character password increases the search space marginally, whereas adding six extra lowercase characters multiplies the combinations by 26⁶—over 308 million times harder to brute-force.

Instead of predictable character substitutions, generate random 4-to-5 word Diceware passphrases separated by punctuation, such as timber-velvet-quarry-compass-48. Drawing from a 7,776-word dictionary gives roughly 12.9 bits of entropy per word, pushing a 5-word passphrase beyond 64 bits of true randomness while remaining effortless to type on mobile devices. GUIDE 02 • IDENTITY SECURITY

FIDO2 Passkeys vs. SMS 2FA: Stopping Adversary-in-the-Middle (AiTM) Phishing

Enabling Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) via SMS text messages or standard 6-digit authenticator apps used to block 99% of automated credential attacks. Today, criminal toolkits such as Evilginx and Tycoon 2FA operate as Adversary-in-the-Middle (AiTM) reverse proxies that bypass traditional 6-digit codes entirely.

When a victim clicks a convincing phishing link, the attacker's server sits transparently between the victim and the legitimate login portal, capturing the authenticated session cookie in real time. FIDO2/WebAuthn passkeys and physical security keys eliminate AiTM phishing through origin binding: your device signs the authentication challenge only if the exact cryptographic domain matches the registered site.

GUIDE 03 • TELECOM & MOBILE SECURITY

How SIM-Swap & Call-Forwarding Hijacks Happen—And How to Lock Down Your Mobile Number

Your mobile phone number acts as a master recovery key for dozens of financial apps and banking portals. In a SIM-swap attack, criminals socially engineer a mobile carrier representative into porting your phone number onto a SIM card or eSIM under their control.

A stealthier variant is unauthorized call and SMS forwarding. Dial *#21# periodically on your keypad to audit active unconditional forwarding rules and ##002# to wipe unauthorized diversions. Always set a dedicated Number Transfer / Port-Out PIN with your mobile carrier.

GUIDE 04 • EMAIL INFRASTRUCTURE

SPF, DKIM, and DMARC Explained: Protecting Domains Against Executive Impersonation

Without cryptographic DNS policies, fraudsters can forge From: headers to send fake wire-transfer requests appearing to originate from your domain. Publishing SPF, DKIM, and an enforced DMARC record (p=reject) instructs global mail providers to block spoofed messages before they reach an inbox. GUIDE 05 • ZERO-TRUST HOME & REMOTE NETWORKS

Hardening Home & Remote-Work Routers: DNS Filtering, Guest Isolation, and UPnP

Disable Universal Plug and Play (UPnP) and WPS in your router administration panel so compromised smart-home gadgets cannot open inbound firewall ports. Place IoT devices onto an isolated Guest Wi-Fi SSID and configure malware-blocking DNS resolvers such as Quad9 (9.9.9.9) or Cloudflare (1.1.1.2).

GUIDE 06 • RANSOMWARE RESILIENCE

The 3-2-1-1-0 Immutable Backup Strategy Against Modern Ransomware

Modern Ransomware-as-a-Service payloads target mapped network drives and synced cloud folders before encrypting local files. Maintain 3 total copies of critical data across 2 storage media types, with 1 copy stored offsite, at least 1 copy kept strictly offline or immutable (Object Lock / WORM storage), and 0 unverified errors through quarterly test restores.

GUIDE 07 • BROWSER & ENDPOINT SECURITY

Defeating Infostealer Malware & Session Token Theft in Modern Web Browsers

Infostealers disguised as cracked software or fake browser updates sweep browser profile folders in seconds to steal saved passwords and active OAuth cookies. Never store high-value banking or cloud passwords inside browser autofill—use an encrypted password manager protected by a hardware key.

GUIDE 08 • FINANCIAL & FINTECH DEFENSE

Compartmentalizing Banking, Virtual Cards, and Recovery Emails

Maintain a dedicated, private email address used exclusively for banking, tax portals, and domain registrars—never enter it on newsletters or public e-commerce sites. Pair online checkouts with single-merchant or spending-capped virtual debit cards so a breached merchant cannot drain your primary account.
